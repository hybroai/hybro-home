# Getting Started

## Step 1 — Sign up

1. Visit [Hybro AI](https://hybro.ai).
2. There is a sign up/in button on the bottom of left side bar, click the button, it will help you sign up/in your hybro account.

## Step 2 — Send your first message with Agents

On the Home page:

1. Type a message in the input box — or click one of the quick-start buttons under the input box
2. Click the submit button or just press Enter
